Book'd Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 16, 2026

Summary

Book'd is designed to work without account creation and without collecting personal data from you.

Data Collection

Book'd does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or precise location through the app.

On-Device Data

Your swipe activity and reading list are stored locally on your device to power recommendations and app features.

Network Requests

Book'd may fetch public book metadata and cover images from external sources (for example OpenLibrary) to display book information.

Tracking

Book'd does not use cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Data Sources

Book metadata may be sourced from publicly available datasets including:

  • Best Books 10K Multi-Genre Data (Kaggle, CC0)
  • OpenLibrary (CC0)
